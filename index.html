Lecture capture and automatic notes

A recorder that runs on a phone during lectures and turns them into finished notes. It uploads in one-minute chunks, transcribes the speech, sorts it by topic, writes a titled daily document and pushes it back to the phone. The whole pipeline runs unattended on a Windows service.

47 lessons into one document  ·  12 daily documents so far  ·  ~91% transcription accuracy

Proves: end-to-end product delivery, audio and speech pipelines, Android plus Python plus backend, shipped to a real user.

Voice assistant, self-hosted

It answers personal questions from my own notes, does live web lookups, reports my location and routes to it, and writes real reminders from voice commands. The phone captures audio, a service on my PC transcribes and answers, and the reply is spoken back.

778 logged exchanges  ·  ~1 second to answer  ·  speech in and out

Proves: API integration, geocoding and routing, speech in and out, iterating on a system until it genuinely works.
